Paid Media – Google Ads & Microsoft Ads22

  • Own the overall strategy, management, monitoring, and optimization of Glendale paid media across Google Ads and Microsoft Advertising.
  • Manage campaign planning across Brand Search, Non-Brand Search, Shopping, Performance Max, Demand Gen, remarketing, promotional, seasonal, and other relevant campaign types.
  • Review daily campaign performance including spend, impressions, clicks, CTR, CPC, conversions, conversion value, ROAS, CPA, search terms, product performance, and budget utilization.
  • Monitor campaign budgets and pacing to ensure spend is aligned with monthly targets, business priorities, promotional periods, and profitability requirements.
  • Review campaign-level, asset-group-level, ad-group-level, keyword-level, product-level, and audience-level performance to identify optimization opportunities.
  • Analyze search terms and implement negative keywords or other targeting refinements where required.
  • Review keyword performance, match types, bids, quality indicators, landing pages, and search intent to improve traffic quality and efficiency.
  • Monitor Performance Max asset groups, audience signals, listing groups, search themes, products, creative assets, and conversion performance.
  • Monitor Shopping and product performance, including high-spend/low-return products, high-converting products, product categories, feed issues, and inventory-related concerns.
  • Coordinate promotional campaigns and ensure offers, promo codes, dates, creative assets, sitelinks, promotions, and landing pages are aligned.
  • Review Google Merchant Center for disapprovals, warnings, feed issues, policy issues, missing attributes, price/availability mismatches, promotions, and product eligibility.
  • Monitor Microsoft Ads campaign performance and maintain parity with the broader paid-media strategy.
  • Review brand campaign performance and ensure brand traffic is captured efficiently without unnecessary budget leakage.
  • Review remarketing activity and audience performance while ensuring messaging remains relevant and aligned with current offers.
  • Monitor Demand Gen/video campaigns for audience quality, creative performance, frequency, traffic quality, and conversion contribution.
  • Review ad copy, headlines, descriptions, assets, extensions, sitelinks, promotions, and landing-page alignment before launch.
  • Ensure paid-media messaging does not use restricted, misleading, unsupported, or policy-sensitive wording.
  • Monitor ad policy issues, account warnings, disapprovals, domain issues, billing issues, suspicious activity, and other account-level risks.
  • Review conversion tracking and investigate sudden changes in conversion volume, revenue, attribution, or campaign behavior.
  • Identify abnormal traffic, suspicious referral patterns, tracking anomalies, unauthorized changes, or unexpected campaign activity and escalate immediately.
  • Maintain a record of major campaign changes, optimization decisions, budget changes, launches, pauses, and strategic adjustments.
  • Prepare paid-media performance summaries and communicate key insights, actions taken, and next steps to the client and internal team.
