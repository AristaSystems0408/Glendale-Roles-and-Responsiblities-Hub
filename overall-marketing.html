1. SujitkumarOverall Marketing Manager

Covers: Overall Marketing · Paid Media · Analytics & Tracking · Website / UI-UX / CRO · Team Management & Quality Control · Reporting & Project Management · Promotions & Campaign Management · Email Marketing

Project Leadership & Marketing Management10

  • Own overall marketing leadership for the Glendale project across Paid Media, SEO, Organic Social Media, Email Marketing, Creative, Website/CRO coordination, Analytics, and client communication.
  • Maintain a complete view of all active marketing initiatives, priorities, dependencies, deadlines, owners, and expected outcomes across the project.
  • Set weekly and monthly priorities for each marketing function based on business goals, campaign performance, seasonality, promotions, inventory, and client requirements.
  • Translate client business objectives into actionable marketing tasks and distribute them to the appropriate team members.
  • Maintain clear ownership for every recurring activity, one-time request, campaign, promotion, creative requirement, SEO task, social deliverable, and email activity.
  • Review the overall project workload and rebalance tasks when a team member is overloaded, unavailable, or blocked.
  • Identify gaps in execution before they become client-facing issues and assign corrective actions with clear ownership and timelines.
  • Maintain continuity of work during holidays, leave, urgent requests, campaign launches, and high-volume promotional periods.
  • Ensure that all marketing activities follow Glendale's brand positioning, business priorities, approved messaging, and internal quality standards.
  • Continuously improve the project process, task flow, review mechanism, communication process, and delivery standards.

Client Communication & Relationship Management10

  • Own day-to-day client communication for marketing-related activities and act as the primary point of coordination.
  • Prepare for and lead client calls, status meetings, review meetings, strategy discussions, campaign discussions, and performance discussions.
  • Collect client feedback, clarify requirements, document decisions, and convert discussions into actionable internal tasks.
  • Follow up on open client requests and ensure that no requirement remains unattended or without a clear owner.
  • Communicate project progress, completed work, pending items, blockers, dependencies, and upcoming priorities to the client.
  • Explain marketing recommendations to the client with business context, expected impact, risks, and implementation requirements.
  • Coordinate client approvals for campaigns, ad copy, creative assets, promotions, landing-page changes, email campaigns, and other activities requiring approval.
  • Maintain professional and timely communication when discussing performance issues, technical limitations, delays, policy restrictions, or corrective actions.
  • Escalate critical issues to management/client stakeholders when required and recommend practical solutions rather than only reporting problems.
  • Maintain a clear record of client feedback and ensure it is reflected in future execution.
